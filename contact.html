Frequently Addressed Inquiries

When in the architectural schedule should STACKLY be engaged? ?

Ideally during the schematic design phase or early design development (DD). Early involvement allows us to preserve ceiling depths for recessed motorized drapery pockets, locate centralized low-voltage equipment closets, and coordinate conduit paths before structural framing begins.

Do you provide stamped electrical conduit submittals? ?

Yes. We engineer comprehensive conduit schedules, cable topology drawings, electrical box rough-in cut sheets, and load schedules directly compatible with your electrical engineering trade partners and local permitting jurisdictions.

Can STACKLY engineer systems for historic and stone structures? ?

Absolutely. Our heritage retrofit engineering deploys ultra-reliable encrypted wireless backbones (Lutron Clear Connect, wireless KNX RF, and fiber-coupled distributed hubs) allowing intelligent control without chasing or compromising historic masonry.

What ongoing warranty and telemetry support do you offer? ?

All STACKLY installations include our 3-Year White-Glove Architectural Guarantee, including 24/7 autonomous bus health telemetry, pro-active equipment replacement, and immediate priority hotline dispatch for the client and estate managers.
